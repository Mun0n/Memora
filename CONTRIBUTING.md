# Contributing to Memora

Thank you for helping build respectful, privacy-first support software.

Before contributing, read `AGENTS.md`, the v0.1 product and architecture documents, and the active slice specification. Work is organized in small slices with explicit acceptance criteria. Do not start a later slice implicitly.

Use only synthetic, clearly labeled fictional data. Do not submit patient records, caregiver details, family photographs, contact information, credentials or production endpoints. Do not make medical efficacy claims or add AI/LLM features without a separately approved specification.

Run `./scripts/validate.sh` before proposing a change. Agent-assisted implementation must use `./scripts/run_slice.sh <number>` and finish at human review.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
